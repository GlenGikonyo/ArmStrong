Our Story

Armstrong Movers & Storage Ltd

Founded in 2008, Armstrong Movers & Storage Ltd has grown from a passionate local startup into one of Kenya's most trusted names in the logistics and relocation industry. With over 18 years of hands-on experience, we have weathered the complexities of the moving industry and emerged as a premier provider of seamless moving solutions across East Africa and beyond.

While our headquarters remain firmly rooted in Kenya, our vision has always been global. We understand that moving is not just about transporting goods; it is about transporting lives, dreams, and businesses. That is why we have dedicated nearly two decades to perfecting the art of stress-free relocation.
