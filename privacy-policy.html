Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Velotrax collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our logistics and freight solutions website.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you contact us, request a freight quote, submit shipment details, create an account, or fill out forms on our website. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company details, billing information, and shipping-related data.

We may also collect limited non-personal information such as browser type, device details, IP address, and general website usage data. This information helps us improve website functionality and overall user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide freight quotes, manage shipment-related requests, offer customer support, and improve our logistics services.

Non-personal data may be used for analytics, website optimization, security monitoring, and fraud prevention. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

  • Information is collected to process logistics inquiries and manage service requests.
  • Usage data helps improve performance, navigation, and user experience.
  • Security measures are implemented to protect website and user data.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers, such as payment processors, IT support providers, or logistics partners, when necessary to operate our website and support service delivery.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect our legal rights and business interests.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality, analyze traffic, and improve user experience. You may manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Template & Demo Notice

This website template may include forms, quote systems, or tracking features for demonstration purposes only. Actual data collection and privacy practices depend on how the final website is configured by the purchaser.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@velotrax.com
