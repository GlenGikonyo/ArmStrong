7 steps of moving

1. First Contact

Call our office. Set a date for our assessor/move coordinator to physically survey your home and prepare an estimate.

2. Assessment Visit

Our assessment agent will come to your home to estimate the volume of your personal effects, free and without obligation.

3. Consultation

Our assessor will be fully prepared to discuss personalized packing services with you, assisting every step of your move.

4. Quote

You receive your free quote without any obligation. Review the quote and book your service after careful review.

5. Pack

Our packing team arrives one day prior to or on the actual moving day, using the industry's best materials to protect your belongings.

6. Transport

All our trucks are enclosed and fitted with tracking technology, ensuring secure and direct routes to your final destination.

7. Unpack

Our professionals unpack your goods, install appliances and carefully reassemble your furniture as per your direction.
