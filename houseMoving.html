Our Process

Our Seven House Moving Steps

1. First Contact

Call our office. Set a date for our assessor/move coordinator to physically survey your home and prepare an estimate.

2. Assessment Visit

Our assessment agent will come to your home to estimate the volume of your personal effects. This visit is free and without any obligation.

3. Consultation

Our assessor will be fully prepared to discuss personalized packing services with you. We are here to assist during every step of your move.

4. Quote

You receive your free quote without any obligation. Review the quote and book your service after careful review.

5. Pack

Our packing team will arrive one day prior to or on the actual moving day and will use the industry's best packing materials to protect your belongings.

6. Transport

All our trucks are enclosed and fitted with tracking technology that ensures secure and direct routes are used to deliver your treasured possessions to their final destination.

7. Unpack

Our professionals unpack your goods, install appliances and carefully reassemble your furniture. Your furniture will be placed according to your direction.
