International Moving and Relocation

Professional International moving
and relocation services.

Moving abroad can be one of life's most challenging experiences. Armstrong's full-service international moving management meets your unique needs when moving families and businesses overseas. We offer different range of freight options depending on your timescale and relocation budget. Armstrong can move your consignment by air, road (Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda and other COMESA countries) or sea. For sea freight, you can choose from a Full Container Load (FCL) or Less than Container Load (LCL)/Consolidated service or a combination of services, which best meet your needs when moving abroad.

The safe arrival of a family's furnishings and treasured possessions in the new location can be a key element in the success of a move. No matter how successful the other aspects of the relocation, if the shipment of the family's goods is undertaken badly it can cause significant stress and negatively impact the employee's view of the entire move.
